About Me

Hi, I am Munna from Khuji Bangladesh Travellers [KBT].

I'm Mahfuzur Rahman Munna, a traveller, tour guide and content creator from Bangladesh. My trips are all in one place I know by heart: the hills, jungle trails and waterfalls of Bandarban.

In 2022 I started filming my trips and sharing them online. The messages kept coming: How much did it cost? How do I get there? Is it safe? Answering them one by one turned into planning trips for people, and that became MR Munna Tour & Travel.

Today I plan and lead treks in Bandarban: Kristong, Rungrangtang, Saka Hapong, Tajingdong, Amiakhum, Nafakhum, the Reserve Forest, the Ruma Circuit, Langlok and the Alikadam Circuit. I've walked every trail I recommend, and I film them, so you see the real trails, stays and food before you book.

My promise is simple: honest prices, safe trekking with local guides, and you talk to me directly on WhatsApp, not a call centre.

Travel Guidance

Smart Tips From Someone Who's Been There.

Permits & ID

  • Carry your NID or passport.
  • Bring several photocopies for checkpoints.
  • Remote trails need a registered local guide.
Ask about permits

Packing

  • Grippy trekking sandals or shoes.
  • Dry bag, power bank and a torch.
  • ORS, basic meds and mosquito repellent.
Get a packing list

Budgeting

  • Travel as a group to share jeep and boat costs.
  • Carry enough cash: remote areas have no ATMs.
  • Keep 10% aside for surprises.
Plan my budget

Best Seasons

  • Waterfalls are at their fullest after the rains.
  • Hill treks: cooler, clearer days in winter.
  • Heavy rain raises streams fast: we check before every trek.

Ask on WhatsApp
BookingHow do I book a tour?

Tell us where you want to go through the trip planner or on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and the final price on WhatsApp, then you secure your seat with an advance payment.

PaymentWhat payment methods do you accept?

bKash, Nagad, bank transfer and cash. Treks need a 50% advance to book transport, guides and stays. The balance is due before departure.

CancellationWhat is your cancellation policy?

Cancel 15+ days before departure for a refund minus non-refundable costs (such as booked tickets). Within 7 days, the advance is non-refundable but you can move it to another date. Full policy is shared before you pay.

PermitsDo you handle permits and checkpoint entries?

Yes. Send us your NID or passport details before the trip and we arrange the entries and a registered local guide. Some trails can close at short notice for weather or security; if that happens we offer another route or a new date.

BookingCan I join a group trip alone?

Absolutely. Many travellers join solo. We can pair you with a same-gender roommate to share room costs.
